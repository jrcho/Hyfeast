    C.2 Flexural Response of an RC Beam: Model Comparison

    Experimental comparison. User-supplied LC1/LC2 RC beam measurements are compared with beam, 2D and 3D continuum models. This case replaces and extends the former C-RCBeam- example. The Excel load convention relative to the drawing's \(P/2\) remains to be confirmed; current values are not classified as completed experimental validation.

    Specimen and models

    The symmetric four-point-bending specimen spans 3,000 mm, is 200 mm wide and 300 mm deep. Support-to-load distances are 1,050 mm for LC1 and 600 mm for LC2.

    Model Representation
    Beam Full span, 16 B2D2H elements, original M2 concrete and reinforcement sections
    2D continuum Half span, CPS4, 30×12 divisions, perfectly bonded embedded reinforcement
    3D continuum Half span, C3D8, 30×12×4 divisions, perfectly bonded embedded reinforcement

    Primary continuum models include stirrups and load the top-surface nodes. Actual loading-plate/contact and three-dimensional stirrup confinement are not separately reproduced. The same specimen is compared across models; their concrete constitutive laws are not identical.

    Material correspondence and elastic checks

    M2 concrete uses modulus 29,164 MPa, Poisson ratio 0.2 and tensile strength 1.9701 MPa. The Maekawa tensile envelope is converted from total strain to the CDP plastic-strain coordinate.

    \[ \sigma_t=f_t\left(\frac{f_t/E}{\varepsilon_t}\right)^{0.4},\qquad \kappa_t=\varepsilon_t-\frac{\sigma_t}{E},\qquad D_t=0. \]

    Compression matches MPPCEnv/MPPCIE strength and residual strain. Initial yield strength is 16.156 MPa and peak strength is 40.39 MPa; the original table ends at total compressive strain 0.004. Constant-strength extension in CDP differs from the original MPPC zero-strength cutoff.

    Uniaxial tension and compression each complete 512 increments without cutback. Maximum relative envelope errors are 0.5819% in tension and 0.0768% in compression; lateral stresses remain below \(10^{-6}\) MPa. Bottom reinforcement retains area 235.6 mm², yield strength 418.68 MPa and post-yield slope 4,870.3 MPa. Top reinforcement retains area 398.2 mm² and perfect plasticity at 492.3 MPa.

    Figure C.2.4 Actual Uniaxial Responses and Original M2 Material Laws

    Elastic checks compare the first increment of section-wide loading with transformed-section stiffness. Beam errors are +0.0030% for LC1 and +0.0023% for LC2. Continuum errors, including stirrup variants, range from −1.294 to −1.006% for LC1 and −1.101 to −0.830% for LC2. All values are preserved in the uniaxial audit and ten initial elastic checks.

    LC1 viscosity and compression-tail sensitivity

    Four additional 2D section-wide loading analyses all complete the target loading-point displacement of 60 mm. They reduce viscosity from \(10^{-5}\) to \(5\times10^{-6}\) or change only the compression tail. The cutoff approximation reduces strength from 22.7106 to 1.00975 MPa over total strain 0.004–0.00405, then holds it constant. This differs from complete zero-strength cutoff in the original MPPC law.

    Table C.2.3 Completed Viscosity and Compression-Tail Sensitivity Analyses

    Completed beam convergence-tolerance diagnostics

    Two initial beam diagnostics using \(10^{-4}\) for both relative force tolerances also terminate normally. Maximum reaction imbalances of 2.462% for LC1 and 0.249% for LC2 exclude them from the primary experimental comparison. Corresponding values for the current primary beam analyses are 0.0301% and 0.0317%.

    Diagnostic input Peak one-point load (kN) Midspan deflection at peak (mm) Maximum reaction imbalance
    beam-loose/beam-lc1-m2 30.686 70.816 2.462%
    beam-loose/beam-lc2-m2 51.587 69.064 0.249%

    Inputs, CSVs, HDBs and logs are restored under diagnosis/beam-loose/; a diagnostic summary is also available. Normal termination does not establish reaction equilibrium. Interrupted strict-tolerance, inviscid and line-search runs are excluded from the completed-case count.

    Inputs and execution

    Data are under Examples/C-RCBeam-ModelComparison/. The original folder preserves experimental workbooks, drawings and former inputs. Current beam inputs are beam-lc1-m2 and beam-lc2-m2. Primary continuum inputs are solid2d-lc1-m2-ties-topload and solid3d-lc1-m2-ties-nols-topload, with corresponding LC2 files.

    Examples\C-RCBeam-ModelComparison\run.bat beam-lc1-m2
Examples\C-RCBeam-ModelComparison\run.bat solid2d-lc1-m2-ties-topload
python Examples\C-RCBeam-ModelComparison\compare.py --topload
python Examples\C-RCBeam-ModelComparison\compare.py
python tools\dev\figures\plot_rcbeam_comparison.py

    Runs use repository-local tmp and copy only successfully completed outputs into the case folder. Rerunning the same input overwrites preserved results.

    The two compare commands process all completed top-loaded and section-wide loading cases, respectively. Existing HDB checks require h5py. Run python tools/dev/figures/publish_cdp_figures.py to publish Help figures and detailed CSVs. The first beam-lc1-m2 execution's HDB, logs and executable hash are separately preserved under original-results/beam-lc1-m2/; its CSV is identical to the current CSV.
